The visit

From a patient presentation to a finished encounter, in one pass.

Complete patient encounters from scheduling to orders in a single workflow. Pre-visit, AI synthesizes the patient's presentation, outside records, and previous encounters into a comprehensive patient timeline. During the visit, AI handles real-time transcription, note drafting, and order parsing while you stay focused on the patient. You review and approve every clinical decision — the AI drafts, the doctor decides.
